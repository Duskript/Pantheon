# FDA-CDS-FAQ — Clinical Decision Support Software Frequently Asked Questions

**URL:** https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/clinical-decision-support-software-frequently-asked-questions-faqs

## FDA-CDS-FAQ-01

> The FDA's guidance titled “Clinical Decision Support Software” provides clarification on the Agency's interpretation of the types of CDS software functions excluded from the definition of a device by the criteria in section 520(o)(1)(E) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (Non-Device CDS criteria).

## FDA-CDS-FAQ-02

> The CDS guidance should not be used as the sole reference; it is one of several guidances that can help determine whether a software function meets the definition of a device.

## FDA-CDS-FAQ-03

> Sponsors can use FDA's Digital Health Policy Navigator, which includes seven steps with questions intended to be answered for each product function, to help guide them to relevant FDA medical device regulatory considerations.
