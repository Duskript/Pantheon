# Travel Gems — Information Gathering Questionnaire

> Purpose: everything Samantha and the website build need, in one pass.
> Format: 45-60 min call, or sent as a form. Erica answers what she can; blanks
> become Phase-2 defaults, not blockers.
> Owner: Konan. Filled copy lives next to this file as `questionnaire-answers.md`.

---

## Section 1 — The business (10 min)

1. How long have you been a Fora advisor, and what did you do before?
2. What's your monthly trip volume today — inquiries, quotes, bookings?
3. Where does your revenue actually come from? (hotel commissions, cruises,
   packages, fees, groups)
4. Which 3 destinations or trip types are your money-makers?
5. Which ones do you want more of? Which do you want to stop taking?
6. Do you charge planning fees? If so, when and how much?
7. Who else works in the business — VA, contractor, partner?

## Section 2 — The traveler (10 min)

1. Describe your ideal client in two sentences.
2. Where do they come from today — referrals, Instagram, Fora leads, repeat?
3. What's the first thing a new inquiry usually says?
4. What do you ask every single client, every time? (This becomes the intake form.)
5. What's the moment clients say "wow"?
6. What's the most common thing that goes wrong on a trip, and how do you handle it?

## Section 3 — Email & calendar (10 min)

1. Which email do you actually live in — Gmail, Workspace, Outlook, Apple Mail?
2. Roughly how many emails a day, and how many need a real response?
3. What are the email types that MUST reach you immediately?
   - client in-destination problem
   - supplier/hotel confirmation or change
   - new inquiry
   - commission/payment notice
   - something else?
4. What can safely wait for a daily summary?
5. Which calendar do you use for work vs personal?
6. How do you schedule client calls now — link, back-and-forth, phone?
7. What hours do you actually work? Which hours are sacred (school pickup, gym,
   no-meetings day)?
8. How far ahead do you prep for a client meeting today, and what do you look at?

## Section 4 — Fora platform & tools (10 min)

1. What do you use inside Fora day to day? (bookings, commissions, hotel search,
   Sidekick/Via)
2. Do you have access to Fora's developer/MCP tooling, or have you seen anything
   about it in the advisor portal? Any docs, API keys, or a settings page?
3. Where do you track travelers today — spreadsheet, notebook, Fora only, memory?
4. What other tools are in your stack? (Canva, Later, Calendly, Notion, QuickBooks,
   TripIt, WhatsApp)
5. What's the one task you'd pay to never do again?

## Section 5 — Brand & website (10 min)

1. Who designed the logo, and do you have the brand kit (fonts, colors, hex codes)?
   *(We have `logo6` in AI/EPS/PDF/SVG/PNG/JPG — all monochrome gray. Is there a
   color version, or is the brand still undecided?)*
2. Three travel brands or websites whose look you love. Three you hate.
3. What should the site do — look credible, capture inquiries, sell a specific
   trip type, host a blog, all of the above?
4. Do you have a domain? Where is it registered? (We have GoDaddy access.)
5. Existing site, Instagram, Facebook page, Google Business Profile — links please.
6. What does a good inquiry look like? What do you want the form to ask?
7. Any Fora compliance rules on your marketing we should follow (logo usage,
   "affiliate of Fora Travel" disclosure, commission claims)?
8. Who writes — you, or do you want drafts?

## Section 6 — Social (5 min)

1. Which platforms matter? (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn)
2. Current cadence — how often do you actually post?
3. What performs? What falls flat?
4. Are you comfortable on camera, or is this faceless/b-roll/static?
5. Where do photos come from — your own trips, client trips, Fora assets, stock?
6. Do you want to be the face of the brand, or should it feel like a company?

## Section 7 — Operations & access (5 min)

1. What timezone are you in? Confirming: `America/Chicago`?
2. What's your tolerance for automation — "draft it and I'll approve" vs
   "just handle it"?
3. How do you want Samantha to reach you — Discord, text, email, WhatsApp?
4. What should she absolutely never do without asking?
5. Who else needs access to any of this?
6. Anything you're worried about with an AI assistant? (Say it now, not in month two.)
