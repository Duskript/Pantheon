## Ad Placement Strategy

| Ad | Audience | Goal |
|----|----------|------|
| **A — $15.6K** | Cold traffic, broad SMB targeting | Hook with the number, drive DM or landing page visit |
| **B — Onboarding** | Service businesses specifically (cleaning, plumbing, HVAC, etc.) | Target by interest + industry, drive to offer doc |
| **C — Social Proof** | Retargeting: people who visited landing page or engaged with Ad A/B | Overcome hesitation, drive conversion |
| **D — Selective** | Decision-makers in $250K–$5M range | Build trust via exclusion, filter out unfit leads |

---

## What to Watch For

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| 5+ DMs in first 72 hours per ad | Hook is landing — increase budget |
| DMs asking "how does it work" | Interested but need clarity → send Offer Doc |
| "That's too expensive" | Price objection → lead with $15.6K saving → $444 is 3% of that |
| Clicks but no DMs | Landing page or CTA isn't clear enough |
| High CTR on Ad A, low on Ad C | Social proof isn't credible enough — get a real case study |

**Social proof gap:** If you have even one anonymized before/after client story, inject it into Ad C and test against Ad A. Real proof beats hook numbers every time.
