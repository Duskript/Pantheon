## THE 7 ADD-ONS (in adoption order)

### 1. Desktop Companion

**What it is:** A tray-resident desktop app that runs in the background on every employee's machine. No windows, no UI. Just a tray icon and a quiet presence.

**What it does for the average firm:**
- Captures every meeting automatically — system audio loopback, local transcription on the device, no cloud calls
- Watches your calendar, knows which meetings are coming up, surfaces the right context 10 minutes before
- Fires native OS toasts when something needs you (deadline, completed workflow, pre-meeting brief) — even when your browser is closed
- Files meeting notes, action items, and deadlines into the right client record automatically

**What it replaces:**
- Granola — meeting notes AI
- Otter.ai — meeting transcription
- Fireflies.ai — meeting notes + CRM sync

---

### 2. Workflow Engine

**What it is:** A visual workflow builder that runs the automations every accounting firm does manually. Pre-built accounting workflows ship day one; clients can build their own.

**What it does for the average firm:**
- Post-meeting workflows: tax review meeting ends → transcript + 3 flagged deadlines + updated checklist, all filed automatically
- Discovery call workflows: lead captured → engagement letter drafted → kickoff scheduled
- Quarterly review workflows: transcript → summary → partner notification
- Audit finding workflows: findings logged → audit trail updated → assigned to senior
- Collections follow-up workflows: invoice aging checked → reminder drafted → owner notified
- Compliance check workflows: deadline approaching → checklist created → assigned

**What it replaces:**
- n8n (self-hosted) — workflow automation
- Zapier — workflow automation
- Manual process time spent on the same automations (hours per week)

---

### 3. Admin (Scheduling + Email)

**What it is:** An AI-powered executive assistant add-on that runs the calendar, the inbox, and the meeting prep — without the cost of a human EA.

**What it does for the average firm:**
- Calendar triage — schedules meetings based on partner preferences, blocks focus time, prevents overbooking
- Meeting prep — pulls the last 3 client interactions, open tasks, and recent notes into a 1-page brief 10 minutes before every meeting
- Email sorting — surfaces what needs a response today, files what doesn't, drafts replies for partner review
- Reminder scheduling — checks in on open tasks at the right time
- Follow-up tracking — every commitment made in a meeting becomes a tracked task

**What it replaces:**
- Part-time virtual assistant
- Calendly
- The hours partners spend triaging email each day
- Manual meeting prep research

---

### 4. Collections

**What it is:** An automated invoice follow-up add-on that handles AR aging without the awkward conversations. Designed for firms that have outstanding receivables they hate chasing.

**What it does for the average firm:**
- Aged invoice detection — flags any invoice 7/14/30/60 days past due
- Graduated reminder drafting — polite, firm, then escalation (no copy-paste, each reminder is contextually aware)
- Owner notification — partners get pinged on accounts that need a human touch
- Write-off flagging — surfaces clients who consistently pay late so the firm can adjust terms
- AR aging reports — automated weekly

**What it replaces:**
- Write-offs (lost revenue) from invoices that never get followed up
- Manual collections time (hours per week for AR-heavy firms)
- Separate AR tools

---

### 5. Client Intake

**What it is:** An automated onboarding add-on that turns a new client sign-up into a fully onboarded engagement. No more 2-5 hours of admin work per new client.

**What it does for the average firm:**
- Welcome packet — branded, personalized, sent automatically on engagement signature
- Document collection — sends the right document request list for the engagement type (tax, audit, advisory, CAS)
- Engagement letter — generated from the engagement type, signed via DocuSeal, archived automatically
- Kickoff scheduling — books the kickoff call with the right team members
- CRM record creation — populates the client record with everything collected
- 30/60/90 day follow-up — checks in at intervals, surfaces any missing data

**What it replaces:**
- 2-5 hours of admin time per new client
- Separate form tools
- Manual document chase emails
- The "did we send the engagement letter?" check-ins

---

### 6. Kairos (Marketing)

**What it is:** A marketing add-on that runs the campaigns accounting firms pay agencies to handle. Built for firms that need to grow, not just run their book.

**What it does for the average firm:**
- LinkedIn campaigns — thought leadership + lead gen, posted on a schedule
- PPC — Google + Bing ads for "accountant near me" + niche terms
- Client reactivation campaigns — emails to dormant clients, segmented by service type
- Social content — posts that draw on actual client work (anonymized)
- SMS campaigns — appointment reminders, deadline nudges

**What it replaces:**
- Marketing agency retainer
- Mailchimp — email campaigns
- Social media scheduler
- The hours spent creating content manually each week

---

### 7. Pantheon Notebook

**What it is:** A research and knowledge add-on that unifies three tools every firm cobbles together — a block-based wiki, a personal notes app, and an AI chat over the firm's documents — into one substrate. Built on the same backend the Pantheon itself uses every day.

**What it does for the average firm:**
- **Institutional layer (wiki-style):** every client engagement, every policy, every workflow, every internal process — lives as a codex page with blocks, databases, and views. Searchable, versioned, multiplayer.
- **Personal layer (scratch-style):** any partner or staff member can capture a thought, a screenshot, a voice memo, a whiteboard photo — and the system auto-tags it, links it to the right client or engagement, and routes it to the right workflow. No manual organizing. Ever.
- **Synthesis layer (chat-style):** ask "what do we know about the Jones return?" in natural language. The system pulls from the firm's actual codexes, actual client records, actual meeting notes — and cites the source of every answer.
- **Daily audio digest:** two voices discuss the firm's open work, the day's priorities, the deadlines — generated automatically each morning. Plays in the partner's car on the way to the office.
- **Agent fleet:** AI assistants that live in the substrate alongside the team. They read the firm's codex, write to it, surface the right context at the right time. Every other AI notebook tool starts at zero corpus; Pantheon Notebook starts with everything the firm has ever done.

**What it replaces:**
- Notion AI — block substrate with AI features
- OneNote — freeform canvas + voice memos + tags
- Notebook LM Pro — RAG chat + audio digest
- AppFlowy — open-source Notion alternative
- Logseq — open-source OneNote alternative
- The shared drive — where the firm's knowledge goes to die

## THE ADOPTION PATH (narrative)

1. **Desktop Companion** — when meetings start multiplying and someone says "wait, who was taking notes?"
2. **Workflow Engine** — when the firm realizes they're paying a person to do what a machine could do
3. **Admin** — when the partner says "I need an EA but I can't afford an EA"
4. **Collections** — when AR aging gets bad enough that someone has to do something
5. **Client Intake** — when the firm hires 2 new people and onboarding becomes a bottleneck
6. **Kairos** — when the firm decides it wants to grow, not just retain
7. **Pantheon Notebook** — when the firm realizes the shared drive isn't cutting it and the wiki is dead and they need the knowledge to actually compound
