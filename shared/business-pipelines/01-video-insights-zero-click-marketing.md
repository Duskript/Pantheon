## Core Takeaway

The old marketing playbook assumed content should drive clicks back to your site. That model is breaking because search, social, marketplaces, and AI answer engines increasingly answer/entertain/retain users without sending traffic away.

The practical answer is not "stop marketing." It is:

> Create understanding, trust, recall, and preference wherever the buyer already is — then capture demand with email signup, branded search, direct replies, and demo/pilot requests.

## Useful Numbers / Claims From The Talk

- Search is not dying; searches are growing, but the share that clicks out is shrinking.
- About **58.5%** of Google searches are zero-click.
- For every 1,000 US Google searches, roughly **360 clicks** go to the open web.
- Meta/Facebook heavily favors posts without external links; platforms want users to stay on-platform.
- Search is now a behavior, not one channel: Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Amazon/eBay, Reddit, ChatGPT, Claude, etc.
- Your "homepage" is now your search result, ChatGPT answer, Reddit thread, LinkedIn post, review theme, YouTube result, marketplace profile, and public proof.

## Action Framework From The Talk

1. Pick **2-3 channels** where the audience actually pays attention.
2. Publish **1-2 zero-click assets per week** with standalone value.
3. Repurpose each idea into native formats.
4. Capture demand: email signup, branded search, demo request, direct replies.
5. Review monthly, repeat weekly, adjust channels quarterly.

## Content As A Service

Every asset needs an internal client/job:

- case study → sales → win rate / cycle time
- how-to guide → customer success → ticket deflection / adoption
- benchmark/report → comms/bizdev → backlinks / invites
- proof post → buyer trust → replies / booked calls

## 10-Field Content Intake

Use before making assets:

1. Client/team served
2. Job to be done
3. Audience / customer profile / stage
4. Desired asset type
5. Moment of use
6. Desired action
7. Source material / subject matter expert
8. Success metric + target
9. Distribution plan
10. Shelf life + refresh trigger

## Portfolio Implication For Konan

This supports Konan's multi-pipeline idea. The right move is not one giant funnel. It is multiple small pipelines that:

- publish useful zero-click deposits,
- capture demand into a list/reply/pilot request,
- measure real buyer behavior,
- promote only the lanes that produce signal.
