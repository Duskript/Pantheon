# Pipeline — AI SEO / GEO / AEO

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** search/discovery service  
**Time budget:** 2 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 7-30 days  

## Offer

Make a business discoverable in Google + AI answers by publishing proof pages, comparison pages, FAQs, and public record assets.

## Target Audience

Local SMBs, niche software/projects, service businesses with weak public proof.

## Weekly Action

Create 1 answer page + 1 comparison/FAQ + 1 proof asset; check whether AI/search mentions improve monthly.

## Validation Event

1 paid $300-$997 visibility audit/setup OR measurable branded-search/AI-answer appearance.

## Failure Signal

No ranking/mention/reply after 30 days and 5 assets; niche too weak or pages not specific enough.

## Primary Metric

Audit sales, indexed assets, branded search, AI answer citations.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
