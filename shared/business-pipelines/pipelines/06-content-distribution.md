# Pipeline — Content Distribution / Second Channel

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** distribution service  
**Time budget:** 2 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 14-30 days  

## Offer

Repurpose one insight into native posts across LinkedIn, X, newsletter, blog, and community posts — no link dependency.

## Target Audience

Founders with ideas stuck in videos/calls/Discord/docs.

## Weekly Action

Take 1 Pantheon/consignment/AI lesson; produce 5 native assets; post 2-3; track reactions/replies.

## Validation Event

10 meaningful engagements OR 1 inbound conversation OR 1 client asks for the same system.

## Failure Signal

3 weeks with no replies or saves; improve topic/angle, not volume.

## Primary Metric

Replies, saves, subscribers, client asks.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
