# Pipeline — AI DM Setting / Inbound Sales

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** conversion service  
**Time budget:** 1.5 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 1-7 days  

## Offer

Turn inbound comments/messages/profile visitors into qualified conversations and booked calls with human-approved replies.

## Target Audience

Creators, local sellers, coaches, SMBs with inbound attention but poor follow-up.

## Weekly Action

Create 3 reply scripts; manually test on own/consignment leads; track response classes and booked intakes.

## Validation Event

5 qualified conversations OR 1 booked intake/client produced by scripted replies.

## Failure Signal

20 conversations with no progression; offer/CTA is wrong.

## Primary Metric

Replies classified, booked calls/intakes, close rate.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
