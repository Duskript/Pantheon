# Pipeline — AI Ads for Meta

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** distribution / creative service  
**Time budget:** 2 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 1-7 days  

## Offer

Create 10 Meta ad concepts + 3 image/video scripts for a small business offer in 48 hours.

## Target Audience

Local SMBs, ecommerce micro-brands, service businesses already posting on Facebook/Instagram.

## Weekly Action

Pick 5 businesses with visible offers; make 1 sample ad angle each; send/DM 3; publish 1 zero-click teardown.

## Validation Event

1 paid $99-$199 creative sprint OR 3 owners ask for samples/pricing.

## Failure Signal

After 20 targeted reaches, <2 replies or no owner recognizes ad creative as a pain.

## Primary Metric

Replies, sample requests, paid sprint.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
