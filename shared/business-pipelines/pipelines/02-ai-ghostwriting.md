# Pipeline — AI Ghostwriting / Founder Voice

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** productized service  
**Time budget:** 2 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 3-10 days  

## Offer

Turn 1 founder voice note/call into 5 LinkedIn/X posts + 1 newsletter draft in their voice.

## Target Audience

Founders/operators who have expertise but inconsistent posting.

## Weekly Action

Collect 3 founder posts/calls/interviews; draft 3 sample posts; publish 2 of Konan/Hermes proof posts; reach 5 founders.

## Validation Event

1 paid $150-$500 content sprint OR 2 founders send source material for a trial.

## Failure Signal

After 25 founders, no source material submitted and no price conversation.

## Primary Metric

Source-material submissions, paid trials, repeat requests.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
