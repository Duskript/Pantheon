# Pipeline — AI Creator + Newsletter List

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** owned audience / compounding asset  
**Time budget:** 2 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 14-45 days  

## Offer

A weekly practical briefing: AI agent workflows, resale automation experiments, and what actually produced money.

## Target Audience

AI operators, solo founders, local SMB automation buyers, people following Pantheon/TheoForge progress.

## Weekly Action

Publish 2-3 zero-click posts; make 5 deposits for every 1 list ask; send 1 weekly email; capture every interested reply.

## Validation Event

25 subscribers OR 5 qualified replies OR 1 inbound paid conversation from newsletter/content.

## Failure Signal

4 weekly issues with <5 subscribers total and zero replies; reposition or switch topic.

## Primary Metric

Subscribers, replies, booked calls, offers clicked.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
