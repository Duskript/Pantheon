# Pipeline — AI Lead Generation

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** prospecting/productized service  
**Time budget:** 2 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 1-10 days  

## Offer

Build a verified, scored prospect list from public demand signals with source URLs and outreach angles.

## Target Audience

Service businesses, agencies, creators, local operators, TheoForge prospects.

## Weekly Action

Build one 15-lead list for one niche; include source proof; test 5 outreach messages manually.

## Validation Event

1 paid $99-$300 lead list OR one list produces a booked conversation for our own offers.

## Failure Signal

3 lists with no usable conversations; targeting/signal criteria are wrong.

## Primary Metric

Hot leads, replies, booked calls, paid list requests.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
