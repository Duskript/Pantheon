# Pipeline — YouTube Agency Lite

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** content ops service  
**Time budget:** 2 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 7-21 days  

## Offer

Turn one long video/interview/webinar into titles, chapters, 5 shorts scripts, and one newsletter issue.

## Target Audience

Founders/creators with existing long-form content but no repurposing system.

## Weekly Action

Process one public video as sample; publish before/after breakdown; reach 5 creators with stale channels.

## Validation Event

1 paid $199-$500 repurposing sprint OR creator sends raw footage/transcript.

## Failure Signal

20 creators reached with no content handoff; market may require proof/case studies first.

## Primary Metric

Content handoffs, paid sprints, retained monthly ops.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
