# Pipeline — Email Monetization + Retention

**Status:** Planning  
**Type:** owned audience + lifecycle revenue  
**Time budget:** 2 hrs/week  
**Time-to-first-dollar:** 14-45 days  

## Offer

Install a simple email list and lifecycle flows that convert interest into replies, purchases, repeat work, and referrals.

## Target Audience

Konan/Pantheon audience first; later clients with dead lists or weak follow-up.

## Weekly Action

Capture subscribers; send 1 weekly field report; build 1 welcome sequence; create 1 client lifecycle template.

## Validation Event

25 subscribers OR 5 replies OR 1 paid opportunity from list/follow-up; for clients, 1 paid setup.

## Failure Signal

4 sends with <20% opens and no replies; topic or CTA too vague.

## Primary Metric

Subscribers, opens, replies, offers clicked, paid conversions.

## Minimum Viable Pipeline

1. Define one concrete audience.
2. Publish or create one zero-click proof asset.
3. Capture interest into one tracker: subscriber, reply, booked call, paid trial, source material, or lead.
4. Make one explicit ask only after useful deposits.
5. Review after 7 days; continue only if there is signal.

## Human Approval Gates

- Any outbound message must be reviewed before sending.
- Any claim of results/proof must be source-backed.
- Any platform account/cookie/session use must be explicitly approved.
- Any spending or paid tooling must be approved.
