# Decisions — 2026-08-27

## “Hard to Kill” song framing
- **Decision:** Title the song **Hard to Kill** and use **You Don’t Get to Quit** as the recurring command/hook.
- **Rationale:** “Hard to Kill” names survival without pretending it is resilience or recovery; “You Don’t Get to Quit” supplies the confrontational voice that cuts through crushing depression.
- **Alternatives considered:** “Not Resilient” was sharper conceptually but less memorable as a title; using “You Don’t Get to Quit” as the title would foreground the command rather than the condition.
- **Evidence:** User selected options 2 and 3 after clarifying the song is about crushing depression in an impossible situation, not resilience.
- **Reversibility:** High; title and hook can be swapped during lyric development without changing the core premise.

## Lyric voice register
- **Decision:** Use more contractions, informal phrasing, and natural slang in the lyric voice and conversational collaboration.
- **Rationale:** A looser register should make the depression feel lived-in and immediate instead of polished or overly literary.
- **Alternatives considered:** Keeping a more formal poetic diction; using heavy slang throughout. Neither fits the desired balance.
- **Evidence:** User explicitly requested more contractions, informal language, and slang.
- **Reversibility:** High; diction can be adjusted line by line during revision.

## Hard to Kill — Pre-Chorus 2
- **Decision:** Lock Pre-Chorus 2 as Option 1, “One Damn Step.”
- **Rationale:** It preserves the original pre-chorus hinge and battle voice while evolving the scene from bed paralysis into the cold shower and the possibility of reaching the street. It adds pressure, not recovery.
- **Alternatives considered:** Options 2–6, including the doorway handle, both shoes at the frame, and a near-identical callback.
- **Evidence:** Konan selected option 1.
- **Reversibility:** High; the section can be swapped locally without disturbing Verse 2 or the chorus.

## Hard to Kill — Chorus 2
- **Decision:** Lock Chorus 2 as Option 4, “Cold Air.”
- **Rationale:** It preserves the chorus identity and command while carrying forward the cold-shower/threshold imagery from Verse 2. “Let the cold air hit you once more” suggests a repeated attempt, not a clean breakthrough.
- **Alternatives considered:** Options 1–3, 5, and 6, including the hand-on-door, collapse-beside-door, one-foot-through-door, no-farther, and even-if-you-stop variants.
- **Evidence:** Konan selected option 4.
- **Reversibility:** High; only the chorus back-half image changes.

## Hard to Kill — Bridge tonal watchpoint
- **Decision:** Revise the bridge line to “A body still here, too tired to fight.”
- **Rationale:** Keeps the physical exhaustion and severity while removing the death-specific escalation.
- **Evidence:** Konan selected Option 4.
