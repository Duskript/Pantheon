## Genre-Lexicon: candidate-probe word boundaries (new tooling pitfall, Apollo)

When probing the corpus for a carried-forward candidate name, a probe that uses `re.escape()` with no
word boundaries returns catastrophic false positives on short candidate names. On 2026-09-23 the
candidate **Mod** returned **876 hits in 163 of 175 files** because the pattern matched inside
"modal", "modern" and "mode" — it ranked first by raw count and was entirely meaningless. The probe
must use `\b…\b` for any candidate of six characters or fewer, or its ranking cannot be read.

Recorded here because the probe is the second method in the two-method selection rule (scan + hand
grep), and a corrupt hand-grep silently degrades the whole selection.
