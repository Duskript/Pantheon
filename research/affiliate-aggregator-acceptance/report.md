# TheoForge — Affiliate Aggregator Acceptance Research Report

**Prepared for:** Konan / TheoForge (B2B SaaS — visual workflow editor / business process automation)
**Date:** July 2026
**Methodology:** Parallel deep research across 3 dimensions — affiliate networks, deal aggregators, and B2B SaaS best practices — synthesized into actionable roadmap.

---

## Executive Summary

The affiliate marketing landscape for B2B SaaS splits into **three distinct categories**, each with a very different entry path:

1. **Affiliate Networks** — Platforms you join as a MERCHANT so affiliates find and promote you. Best for B2B SaaS: **Rewardful ($49/mo)** for immediate launch, **PartnerStack ($1K/mo)** for scale. Traditional nets (ShareASale, CJ, Rakuten) are consumer-oriented and a poor fit.

2. **Deal Aggregators** — Sites that sell your product at a discount and pay you a revenue share. Best bets: **G2/Capterra/GetApp** (free credibility profiles), **Dealify/PitchGround** (easier entry), **AppSumo** (highest traffic but ~5% acceptance rate).

3. **Direct Affiliate Program** — You run it yourself via a tracking tool. **This is the fastest path to revenue.** Launch in hours on Rewardful, recruit affiliates yourself.

**Key insight:** The workflow automation space has a gaping opportunity — Zapier has no cash affiliate program, n8n pauses applications periodically, Make's is solid but not exceptional. A well-designed TheoForge program at 30% recurring for 12 months could become the #1 program in this space overnight.

## Part 2: Deal Aggregators — Listing & Acceptance Guide

### Three Categories

#### Category A: Review/Directory Platforms (Free, Essential Credibility)

| Platform | Action Required | Acceptance | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G2** | Create free profile | Very high (conditional approval) | 20-30M visits/mo; essential for B2B buyers |
| **Capterra** | Create free profile | Very high | Strong SEO; syndicates to GetApp + Software Advice |
| **GetApp** | Auto-syndicated from Capterra | Auto | Free additional coverage |
| **SaaSWorthy** | Create free listing | High | Directory presence |
| **TrustRadius** | Create free profile | High | B2B review platform |

**Strategy:** These are table stakes — create free profiles immediately. They drive credibility and organic discovery, not direct affiliate revenue. One Capterra profile = three directories (Capterra + GetApp + Software Advice).

#### Category B: Deal Marketplaces (Revenue-Capable, Selective)

| Platform | Acceptance Rate | Vendor Split | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AppSumo** | ~5% | ~30% to you (70% to AS) | Highest traffic; very selective; LTD model |
| **Dealify** | Moderate | Vendor-friendly (no public %) | Easier entry; flexible terms |
| **PitchGround** | Moderate | Commission-based | Targets early-stage SaaS explicitly |
| **DealMirror** | Moderate | Up to 70% to you | Smaller but fairer split |
| **SaaSPirate** | Moderate | Unclear (likely affiliate) | Weekly newsletter distribution |
| **StackSocial** | Lower barrier | ~50% to you | Less ROI per indie hacker reports |

**Key Findings:**
- **AppSumo** is the whale but the hardest to catch. ~5% acceptance is real. Tons of established products get rejected. Don't build strategy around it alone.
- Deal sites demand **50-90% discounts** off normal pricing. For TheoForge, a "Founder's LTD" at $99–$297 would be typical. Normal pricing must be established first to make the discount credible.
- **Dealify** and **PitchGround** are your best Tier 1 targets for deal listings — easier entry, more vendor-friendly terms.

#### Category C: Launch/Discovery Platforms (Credibility Gateways)

| Platform | Action | Direct Revenue? |
|---|---|---|
| **Product Hunt** | Strategic launch | No — credibility & user acquisition |
| **BetaList** | Pre-launch listing | No — early audience building |

**Strategy:** Product Hunt is the gateway to deal sites. Products with PH traction get AppSumo attention. It's not a revenue channel — it's a proof-of-concept channel.

## Part 3: B2B SaaS Affiliate Program Best Practices

### TheoForge Recommended Blueprint

| Decision | Recommended Starting Point | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Commission** | 30% recurring for 12 months | Matches n8n; slightly below Make (35%); competitive |
| **Cookie duration** | 90 days (180 if margins allow) | B2B sales cycles require longer windows |
| **Payout threshold** | $100 | Standard SaaS minimum |
| **Payout frequency** | Monthly, Net-30 | Industry standard |
| **Payment methods** | PayPal + Bank Transfer + Wise (intl) | Covers most affiliates |
| **Tiers** | Start flat → add tiers after 10-20 affiliates | Don't overcomplicate at launch |
| **Tracking** | **Rewardful** | Stripe-native, quickest setup, 0% trans fee |
| **Assets** | Comparison chart, demo video, 3 banners, email templates, workflow templates | Comparison charts are #1 asset for workflow tools |
| **Legal** | FTC disclosure clause, cookie consent, affiliate T&Cs | Non-negotiable compliance |
| **Recruiting** | Competing workflow tool affiliates (n8n/Make/Zapier promoters) → content creators in automation niche | Highest ROI for outreach |

### Competitive Landscape (Workflow Automation)

| Tool | Commission | Structure | Cookie | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Make.com** | 35% recurring | 12 months | 90 days | Strongest direct competitor |
| **n8n.io** | 30% recurring | 12 months | ~90 days | **Pauses applications periodically** |
| **Notion** | Up to $50 + 20% Y1 rev OR 50% for 12mo | Hybrid | 180 days | Very generous |
| **Zapier** | ❌ No cash affiliate program | N/A | N/A | **Opportunity gap** |
| **Airtable** | $10 credit (not cash) | One-time | N/A | Weak program |
| **HubSpot** | 30% recurring | 12 months | 90+ days | Gold standard (400+ assets) |

**The Opportunity:**
Zapier has NO cash affiliate program. n8n pauses applications. Airtable offers only credits. TheoForge at 30% recurring with a well-designed program could quickly become the **most attractive affiliate offer in the workflow automation space.**

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Launching too early** — If no one's talking about TheoForge organically, don't launch an affiliate program yet. It amplifies existing advocacy, it doesn't create it.
2. **Weak commissions** — Below 20% recurring, affiliates won't prioritize you.
3. **No tracking trust** — Self-serve dashboard with live stats is non-negotiable.
4. **Complex onboarding** — Apply and approve in < 24 hours or you lose them.
5. **No creative assets** — Affiliates need comparison charts, banners, email templates. They're content creators, not designers.
6. **No fraud prevention** — Block self-referrals, PPC on branded keywords, and suspicious patterns from day one.

## Part 4: Phased Roadmap for TheoForge

### Phase 0 — Foundation (Week 1, $0 cost)
- [ ] Create **free G2 profile** → auto-credibility
- [ ] Create **free Capterra profile** → syndicates to GetApp + Software Advice
- [ ] Create **SaaSWorthy** and **TrustRadius** free profiles
- [ ] Start collecting user reviews (seed with power users)
- [ ] Write affiliate program **Terms & Conditions**
- [ ] Update **privacy policy** for affiliate tracking cookies
- [ ] Implement **cookie consent banner** (GDPR)

### Phase 1 — Launch Affiliate Program (Week 1-2, $49/mo)
- [ ] Sign up for **Rewardful** ($49/mo, 14-day free trial)
- [ ] Configure: 30% recurring for 12 months, 90-day cookies, $100 min payout
- [ ] Connect **Stripe integration** for automated tracking
- [ ] Create **affiliate landing page** explaining the program
- [ ] Prepare comparison chart (TheoForge vs. n8n vs. Make)
- [ ] Prepare 3 email templates, 3 banner sizes, demo video
- [ ] Pre-built workflow templates as showcase assets
- [ ] Add "Become an Affiliate" link in footer + post-signup flow

### Phase 2 — Recruit Affiliates (Weeks 2-4, $49/mo)
- [ ] **Target priority #1:** Affiliates already promoting n8n, Make, Zapier
  - Search YouTube for "n8n review" → check descriptions for affiliate links
  - Search Google for "best workflow automation tools" → contact authors
  - Search X/Twitter for affiliate links to competing products
- [ ] **Target priority #2:** Content creators in automation/no-code niche
  - Bloggers covering business automation
  - Newsletter writers in SaaS space
  - YouTube tutorial creators making workflow content
- [ ] Cold outreach: expect 50-100 outreaches for 5-10 active affiliates
- [ ] Enable "Affiliate Finder" tool in Rewardful

### Phase 3 — Deal Aggregators (Month 2-3)
- [ ] **Product Hunt launch** (strategic, build audience first)
  - Best time: 12:01 AM PT, engage all day
  - Prepare maker comment, video demo, strong visuals
  - Get a "Hunter" from the community if possible
- [ ] If PH goes well → **apply to AppSumo** via Partner Portal
- [ ] Submit to **Dealify**, **PitchGround**, **DealMirror**, **SaaSPirate**
  - Prepare LTD pricing (e.g., Founder's Plan at $99–$297)
  - Normal pricing must be well-established first

### Phase 4 — Scale (Month 3-6+)
- [ ] When affiliate revenue exceeds $10K/mo → evaluate **PartnerStack** (~$1K/mo)
  - Access to 116K+ B2B SaaS partners
  - Startup discount may be available
- [ ] If additional reach needed → test **Awin Access** ($49/mo + 3.5%)
- [ ] Introduce **affiliate tiers** after 10-20 active affiliates
- [ ] Implement **affiliate-refer-affiliate** program for self-sustaining growth
